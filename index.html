Winter Heating & CO Hazards

Zero-Emission Heating Rule:

Never burn coal, wood, or use unvented kerosene heaters inside sealed winter rooms. Silent Carbon Monoxide (CO) poisoning spikes drastically during winter inversions.

Electric Oil-Filled Radiator (OFR):

Recommended for sealed bedrooms. Does not burn oxygen, produce combustion soot, or strip air moisture compared to cheap glowing coil heaters.

Avoid Incense & Mosquito Coils:

Burning 1 mosquito coil produces the equivalent PM2.5 of 75-100 cigarettes in a closed room. Use electric liquid vaporizers or mechanical nets instead.

Beware Ionic "Purifiers"

The Ground-Level Ozone Trap:

Beware of purifiers marketed as "ionic", "plasma", or "ozone generators". Ozone is a potent lung irritant that permanently scars alveoli tissue and worsens asthma.

Gold Standard HEPA Only:

True H13/H14 HEPA mechanical filtration relies on fiber interception and impaction — it emits ZERO secondary chemistry or ozone.

Hydration & Mucosal Barrier:

Drink 2.5–3L warm fluids daily. Maintain 40–50% indoor relative humidity to keep the nasal mucociliary escalator functioning to sweep particulates out.

DIY Corsi-Rosenthal Box Air Cleaner

A proven open-source air purifier invented by environmental engineers. Delivers 500+ CFM CADR (outperforming many $350 commercial purifiers) for under $30-$40.

Required Materials:
  • 1× 20" Box Fan (or standard square ventilation fan)
  • 4× MERV-13 (or MPR 1900+) furnace filters (20" × 20" × 1" or 2")
  • 1× Cardboard bottom shroud + Duct tape
Assembly Steps:
  1. Form a 4-sided cube using the 4 filters (airflow arrows pointing INWARD).
  2. Tape cardboard to form the solid bottom base.
  3. Mount the box fan on top with air blowing UPWARD.
  4. Add a 15" circular cardboard shroud on top fan face to prevent corner recirculation.
